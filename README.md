# Ghost Dice — Privacy Policy

This repository hosts the official Privacy Policy for the **Ghost Dice** application.

---

## 🔒 Privacy Overview

Ghost Dice is designed with privacy as a priority:

- **Zero Data Collection**: No personal information, identifiers, or tracking data is collected.
- **100% Offline**: The application operates completely offline without internet connectivity.
- **Local Storage Only**: All game data is stored strictly on your local device.
- **No Ads or Tracking**: No third-party analytics, tracking services, or advertisements.
- **Family Safe**: Compliant with global privacy standards, including COPPA and GDPR.
