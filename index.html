Section 1

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Ghost Dice is an offline tabletop companion and automated digital Ludo board designed for 2 to 4 players gathering around a single shared physical device.

Our philosophy is straightforward: Physical Dice. Digital Board. Zero Surveillance. Players roll real, physical dice on the table and tap their rolled numbers into the app, which handles token calculations, safe square rules, captures, pair shields, and fair democratic challenge voting.

Because Ghost Dice is intended strictly as a local pass-and-play experience, we have zero interest in collecting your personal data. We do not require accounts, we do not sync your gameplay to remote servers, and we never monitor your activity.

Section 2

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Ghost Dice does not gather, intercept, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, avatars, or physical addresses.
  • No Account Credentials: There is no user registration, sign-in, password management, or social media integration.
  • No Location Data: Ghost Dice does not access GPS, Wi-Fi location, or IP-based geolocation.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not read or record IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, Advertising IDs (IDFA/GAID), or hardware serial numbers.
  • No Media, Photos, or Contacts: The app has zero access to your address book, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not record session length, user interactions, or screen taps for marketing or analytics.
Section 3

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a seamless gaming experience (such as resuming an interrupted match or remembering your favorite house rules), Ghost Dice saves small configuration files strictly on your local device storage:

Data Item Technology Used Purpose Network Transmission
Active Match State Local SQLite Database (sqflite) Saves token positions, current turn, and roll history to enable "Continue Game" if the app is closed or minimized. Never Transmitted
Player Nicknames Local Storage / SharedPreferences Temporarily remembers custom names entered on the Match Setup screen during a local game session. Never Transmitted
House Rule Preferences Local SharedPreferences Stores rule toggles (e.g., exact roll to enter, star square immunity, three-sixes penalty). Never Transmitted
Audio & Settings Local SharedPreferences Remembers sound effect toggles (on/off) across sessions. Never Transmitted

Notice: All of the above data resides exclusively in your device's private app sandbox. We have no mechanism or desire to access this data.

Section 4

4. Device Permissions

Ghost Dice is designed to be as non-intrusive as possible. It runs without requesting sensitive Android or OS permissions:

  • No Internet Permission: The app does not request android.permission.INTERNET. It cannot communicate over networks even if it tried.
  • No Storage Permissions: Does not request broad read/write access to external storage or photos. Data is restricted to standard internal app storage.
  • No Background Services: Does not run persistent background processes or battery-draining telemetry.
Section 5

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Many mobile apps integrate third-party software development kits (SDKs) that silently collect behavioral data. Ghost Dice takes an absolute stance:

  • No Advertising SDKs: We do not display banner ads, video interstitials, or native ads. No ad networks are integrated.
  • No Analytics Platforms: We do not embed Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Flurry, Mixpanel, or similar tracking tools.
  • No Third-Party Brokers: We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any data with data brokers or marketing firms.
Section 6

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Ghost Dice is a family-oriented tabletop game companion enjoyed by players of all ages, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

Because our application does not collect, request, or transmit any personally identifiable information from anyone, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Parents and guardians can feel completely confident allowing their children to play Ghost Dice.

Section 7

7. Data Security & Deletion

Since all data is saved locally on your hardware, its security is protected by your device’s built-in operating system security features (passcode, biometric locking, and app sandboxing).

You maintain 100% control over your data at all times:

  • Resetting Data: You can wipe all game matches and preferences at any time by selecting "Clear Storage" or "Clear Data" for Ghost Dice in your device's Application Settings.
  • Uninstalling: Completely uninstalling the app immediately and permanently erases all locally saved game files and settings from your device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Any changes will continue to uphold our core commitment: Ghost Dice will remain an offline, tracker-free tabletop companion.
